RR-03 SURVIVED on its first run. The mutation then made the adapter return a transport
failure without reading only for the kinds outside `SAFE_TO_REPLAY_FAILURE_KINDS`, and
no test drove a transport failure through a rotation. Looking at why exposed a real gap:
the adapter returned the "safe" kinds unread, and `SafeHttpClient` reports a socket that
died after the request was written as `UNREACHABLE`, one of those kinds. The adapter now
reads back after every transport failure, both fakes gained a `rotates-then-drops`
mode, and the row above is the re-run against that code. RR-01 and RR-02 were re-run
after the change too.

RR-18 to RR-20 are the fixes for the one Codex review of PR #62, each reverted alone
against the test written for it. RR-09 was re-run after its test was renamed.

The ABANDONED refusal for an unperformable operation type has no row: with
`ROTATE_SUBSCRIPTION` performable, no contract type reaches it. See the amendment to
F4E-06..08 in `docs/phase4e-falsification.md`.
