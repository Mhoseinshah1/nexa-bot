Presentation only: every query, mutation, permission prop, confirm dialog, reason field and validation is the page's own, unchanged.
The money cards (receipt review, reconcile, late-payment decision, refunds, wallet credit) are not edited: the quick-action bar only
jumps to them. Kit pieces used: `QuickFilters`, `SelectionBar`, `QuickActionBar`/`QuickAction`, `AttentionStrip`, `KpiRow`, `CardRows`,
`Disclosure`, `PageHead` (`freshness`).
